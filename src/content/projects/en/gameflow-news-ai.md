## About the Project

**GameFlowNews.Ai** is a weekly curated newsletter that filters, summarizes, and provides in-depth analysis on the most impactful Artificial Intelligence developments in the video game industry.

In a landscape of rapid technological shifts, the project cuts through the noise, delivering structured, actionable insights for game developers, artists, QA engineers, marketers, and studio leaders.

## Editorial Structure

Curation, writing, and publication are organized across **5 dedicated tracks** plus a special research section:

- **Product & Game Design**: Mechanics innovation, generative game design, and AI-driven player experience.
- **Design & Art**: 2D/3D asset generation, concepting, texturing, rigging, animation, and creative toolchains.
- **Development & Engineering**: Code generation, autonomous NPC agents, engine plugins, and runtime optimization.
- **QA & Automated Testing**: AI playtesting bots, anomaly detection, automated balancing, and QA pipelines.
- **Marketing & UA (User Acquisition)**: Generative creatives, predictive analytics, ad campaigns, and market intelligence.
- **Research & AI**: Latest academic papers, foundation models, and frontier techniques in game AI.
